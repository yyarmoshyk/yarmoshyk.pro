I design and lead the implemetation of production-ready `Google Cloud Platform` — resource hierarchy, networking, identity and Kubernetes — implemented as Terraform infrastructure-as-code and provide consultations about your existing setup.

### What I help with on GCP

- **Landing Zone & resource hierarchy** — org/folder/project structure following Google's own Landing Zone guidance
- **Networking & IAM** — VPC design, shared VPC, least-privilege IAM ready for enterprise and compliance requirements
- **GKE** — cluster architecture, upgrades and operational standards for Kubernetes on GCP
- **Infrastructure as Code** — Terraform/Terragrunt frameworks for repeatable, auditable infrastructure changes
- **CI/CD** — GitHub Actions or GitLab CI pipelines for infrastructure and application delivery
- **Cost optimization** — finding the architectural decisions driving unnecessary GCP spend

### Why work with me

**6 years of real-world production experience with GCP**, backed by the [Google Cloud Certified – Professional Cloud Architect](https://www.credly.com/badges/3f6c1893-8c42-4e4d-bd64-78e7d694e868/public_url) certification — Google Cloud's top-tier architecture credential.

14 years as a systems and DevOps engineer, focused on turning hard-won production lessons into standards that hold up. I take ownership of the cloud platform problem so your engineering team can stay focused on the product.
