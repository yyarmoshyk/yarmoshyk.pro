Most startups adopt AWS Well-Architected principles unevenly — some pillars get attention, others are ignored until an incident or an enterprise customer's security questionnaire forces the issue. I run a review scoped to your actual workload and turn it into a prioritized, actionable and specific roadmap instead of a generic checklist.

### How the review works

- **Assess** — walk through your workload against the six pillars: operational excellence, security, reliability, performance efficiency, cost optimization and sustainability
- **Prioritize** — separate what's genuinely risky from what's theoretical, based on your stage and business constraints
- **Roadmap** — a sequenced plan with effort estimates, not a dump of AWS's own checklist
- **Implement** — I can lead the remediation work directly, or hand it to your team with clear standards to follow

### Why this matters for startups

Enterprise customers and compliance audits (SOC 2, ISO 27001) increasingly expect evidence of Well-Architected practices. Doing this early avoids the expensive rework of retrofitting security and reliability into infrastructure that was only ever built to be "good enough for the MVP."
