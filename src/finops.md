	<p class="lead">The more you save today, the less you'll overpay tomorrow.</p>
</div>

Cloud bills rarely grow because of one bad decision — they grow because small architectural shortcuts compound month over month: oversized instances, unused resources left running, no autoscaling, data transfer patterns nobody accounted for. I find where your AWS or GCP spend is actually going and fix the decisions driving it, not just the line items.

### What's covered

- **Cost visibility** — break down spend by service, environment and team so you know what's actually driving the bill
- **Architectural review** — identify the design decisions (not just idle resources) that are structurally expensive
- **Right-sizing & commitment planning** — compute, storage and database sizing, plus Reserved Instances/Savings Plans or Committed Use Discounts where they make sense
- **Waste elimination** — unused resources, orphaned volumes, over-provisioned environments
- **Guardrails** — budgets, alerts and tagging standards so cost creep gets caught early instead of at the next invoice

### Who this is for

Startups and growing companies whose AWS or GCP bill has started growing faster than usage, or who want cost put on a sustainable footing before it becomes a board-level conversation.
