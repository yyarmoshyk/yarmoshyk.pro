Most startups reach a point where the infrastructure that got them to product-market fit starts working against them — no clear environments, no IaC, brittle deploys, and no answer for the security questionnaire that just landed from an enterprise prospect. I build the platform layer so that problem goes away.

### What the platform includes

- **Cloud foundation** — AWS or GCP Landing Zone, networking and identity sized for where you're headed, not just where you are
- **Infrastructure as Code** — Terraform/Terragrunt so infrastructure changes are reviewed, repeatable and auditable
- **Kubernetes, if it earns its complexity** — introduced only when it solves a real problem, not by default
- **CI/CD** — GitHub Actions or GitLab CI pipelines your team can extend
- **Observability & operational standards** — the baseline needed to run the platform without guesswork

### Who this is for

CTOs and engineering leads at post-seed and growing startups who need someone to take ownership of the cloud platform problem — not another DevOps engineer to maintain a ticket backlog. I design the architecture, build the foundation, and can either hand it off with clear standards or stay on in a Center of Excellence model for ongoing support.
